#4471 is closed, but the one-day problem is not. Neither the parser fix nor retiring the form touches it. Keep its reports on its own ticket.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser read dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April went out as 4 July. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: the booking-v3 form asked for the wrong order. Its date field said MM/DD/YYYY, so people typed month first, and the form then read the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date came out flipped the same way, so the reports looked like parser, but they came only through the form on booking-v3 tenants. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports can come this way.

one-day: the date moves by one day. The invite lands one day early, or one day apart between clients in different places (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). A month/day flip cannot do that, so the parser fix does not end it. The desk had noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but filed them under parser by tenant. Open.

How the reports were counted

  • By id: the export repeats some reports across pages, so a repeat is counted once.
  • By tenant and channel, not by the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and is often wrong about which form the report came through.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move counts under one-day, and also under its tenant’s cause, where the desk filed it.

Lessons

  • Matching on subject (decision 0001) merged three problems into one ticket. Read the bodies before you call a big ticket known.
  • Closing a merged ticket closes only the problems its fix touches. Check each one.
