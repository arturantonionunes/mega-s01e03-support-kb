The parser fix does not close #4471. The other two problems keep coming in after it ships.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April goes out as 4 July. This is the problem #4471 names.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. Its date field says MM/DD/YYYY, so people type month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date comes out flipped the same way, so the reports look like parser, but it happens only through the form on booking-v3 tenants, and the parser fix does not touch it.

one-day: the date moves by one day. The invite lands one day early, or one day apart between clients in different places (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). A month/day flip cannot do that. The desk already noted these reports (desk-notes.json), but filed them under parser by tenant.

How the reports were counted

  • Each report once: the export repeats some reports across pages, so it is counted by id.
  • By tenant and channel, not by the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and is often wrong about which form the report came through.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move counts under one-day, not under its tenant’s cause.

Lessons

  • Matching on subject (decision 0001) merged three problems into one ticket. Read the bodies before you call a big ticket known.
